Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

sndmail is a desktop email client. It runs on your computer and talks to your mail providers directly. There is no sndmail server: we never receive, store or see your mail, contacts, calendar or account credentials.

What sndmail accesses

When you add a Google account, sndmail asks Google for permission to read, send and organise your Gmail, manage your send-as aliases, and read and write your Google Calendar. It uses that access only to show your mail and calendar in the app and to carry out what you do there — sending, archiving, labelling, deleting, creating events and so on. For other providers it uses IMAP, SMTP and CalDAV with the credentials you enter.

Where your data is kept

Messages, contacts, calendar events, settings and sign-in tokens are stored in a local database on your computer. Passwords and tokens are encrypted, with the key held in your operating system's credential store. Removing an account deletes its local data; uninstalling sndmail and deleting its data folder removes everything.

What leaves your computer

  • Your mail providers (Google, Microsoft, Yahoo, iCloud, your IMAP server…) receive the requests needed to sync and send your mail and calendar.
  • AI features are off unless you turn them on. If you add your own API key for Anthropic, OpenAI or Google Gemini, or point sndmail at a local model, the text of the mail you ask about is sent to that provider under your account with them. Nothing is sent to an AI provider you have not set up.
  • Sender pictures: sndmail may look up a sender's Gravatar (by a hash of their address) and a company's icon (by its domain).

sndmail does not sell or share your data, does not use it for advertising, and has no analytics or tracking.

Google user data

sndmail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide sndmail's user-facing features, is never transferred to anyone except as described above at your request, and is never used for advertising or to train AI models. You can revoke sndmail's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
